# Disclaimer

**Restaurant Location Intelligence** is an independent portfolio and research
project by Digvijay Singh. It demonstrates data engineering, geospatial
analysis and financial modelling on open data.

## Not advice

Everything the app and this repository produce is a **model estimate for
illustration**. That includes scores, rankings, revenue, costs, contribution,
break-even, capex, payback, verdicts and risk flags. It is **not** financial,
investment, real-estate, legal, tax or professional advice of any kind. Do
not rely on it to sign a lease, invest, lend or make any other decision.
Always verify on site and with qualified professionals.

## Known limitations

The model's assumptions and limitations are documented in
[docs/methodology.md](docs/methodology.md#limitations). In particular:
- foot traffic is proxied from static points of interest;
- rent figures are author estimates calibrated to public market-report ranges;
- neighbourhood crime variation is an author estimate (municipal rates are official);
- the demand model has not been backtested against real openings and closures;
- licence/heritage flags are approximate.
